About the Project

FBLA Website Design Competition

Project Purpose

The "Journey Through History" website was developed as an educational resource for high school students and educators. Its primary goal is to provide a visually engaging and interactive overview of the transformative centuries between 1400 and 1700. By focusing on three distinct eras, the site helps users understand the continuity and change that defined the transition from the medieval world to the modern age.

Design Philosophy

The design of this website is inspired by historical manuscripts and the concept of a "living history" book. The color palette utilizes parchment tones and deep ink colors to evoke a sense of antiquity, while the clean layout and responsive design ensure accessibility for modern users. Key technical features include:

  • Responsive Layout: Optimized for desktops, tablets, and mobile devices.
  • Interactive Timelines: Dynamic content delivery using JavaScript.
  • Semantic HTML5: Ensuring accessibility and proper document structure.
  • Visual Hierarchy: Clear navigation and readable typography for educational value.

Target Audience

This site is tailored for:

  • Students: Seeking a concise and interactive summary of historical periods.
  • Educators: Looking for a supplemental visual aid for classroom instruction.
  • History Enthusiasts: Interested in a curated look at pivotal centuries.
